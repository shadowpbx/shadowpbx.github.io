### Core Concepts

#### Major Perspectives
*   **Structural Functionalism**: Views society as a complex system whose parts work together to promote solidarity and stability.
*   **Conflict Theory**: Views society as a site of inequality and struggle for resources and power.
*   **Symbolic Interactionism**: Focuses on micro-level interactions and how symbols create shared meaning.
