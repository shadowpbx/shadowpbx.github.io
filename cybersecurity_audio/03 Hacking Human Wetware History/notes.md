By understanding historical cons, security professionals can recognize the exact same psychological levers used in modern spear-phishing, deepfake vishing, Business Email Compromise (BEC), and zero-day social exploits.


## 🏛️ Category 1. Authority, Impersonation & Credential Spoofing

> **Psychological Lever:** *Trigger: Blind obedience to uniforms, titles, and perceived power.*

- **The Captain of Köpenick (1906):** Shoemaker buys a military uniform and confiscates a town's treasury. *(Cyber: Physical tailgating / Badge spoofing)*
- **Frank Abagnale’s Cons (1960s):** Forging pilot/doctor credentials to exploit corporate trust. *(Cyber: Credential forging / Insider impersonation)*
- **Cassie Chadwick (1890s):** Impersonated Andrew Carnegie’s daughter to extract bank loans. *(Cyber: VIP Impersonation / Whale Phishing)*
- **Ferdinand Waldo Demara (1950s):** "The Great Impostor" forged documents to work as a surgeon and prison warden. *(Cyber: Identity theft / Resume fraud)*
- **Isabella Stewart Gardner Heist (1990):** Thieves bypass elite museum security simply by wearing fake police uniforms. *(Cyber: Rogue IT/Helpdesk impersonation)*
- **Gilbert Chikli’s "Le Président" (2005):** Chikli pioneered calling branch managers, claiming to be the CEO or a secret agent, demanding immediate, secret wire transfers. *(Cyber: The origin of modern Business Email Compromise/CEO Fraud)*
- **Anthony Gignac (1988–2018):** Impersonated a Saudi Prince for 30 years to live a luxury life and defraud investors. *(Cyber: Advanced Persistent Threat (APT) via social engineering)*
- **Operation Mincemeat (1943):** British intelligence planted fake top-secret documents on a corpse in a military uniform to trick the Nazis. *(Cyber: Spoofed sender / Disinformation & Decoys)*
- **Princess Caraboo / Mary Baker (1817):** A cobbler's daughter faked an exotic language and convinced an English town she was foreign royalty. *(Cyber: Fabricated digital footprints / Catfishing)*
- **Christophe Rocancourt (1990s):** Scammed the elite by moving to Hollywood and convincing them he was a French nobleman or a Rockefeller heir. *(Cyber: Affinity fraud via fabricated OSINT)*

## 🏛️ Category 2. Greed, Exclusivity & Advance-Fee Fraud

> **Psychological Lever:** *Trigger: The desire for sudden wealth and willingness to ignore logic for a "secret deal."*

- **The Spanish Prisoner (1800s):** Letters asking for upfront cash to release a wealthy prisoner and his hidden treasure. *(Cyber: Nigerian 419 / Advance-fee fraud)*
- **Victor Lustig Sells the Eiffel Tower (1925):** Exploiting corporate secrecy to sell public infrastructure for scrap. *(Cyber: Fraudulent vendor billing)*
- **George C. Parker Sells the Brooklyn Bridge (1900s):** Setting up fake toll booths and selling public monuments to eager immigrants. *(Cyber: Phishing pages for non-existent assets)*
- **The Wash-Wash / Black Money Scam (1990s):** Convincing victims that black paper is dyed currency requiring expensive chemicals to clean. *(Cyber: Crypto recovery scams / "Gas fee" fraud)*
- **Charles Ponzi’s Scheme (1920):** Arbitrage on international reply coupons; paying early investors with new investors' money. *(Cyber: Yield farming Ponzi / DeFi rug pulls)*
- **Bernie Madoff (1990s–2008):** The ultimate evolution of the Ponzi scheme, relying on exclusive "black box" trading algorithms. *(Cyber: Black-box crypto staking platforms)*
- **The Sir Francis Drake Estate (Oscar Hartzell, 1920s):** Scammed thousands by claiming he was unlocking Drake's lost pirate fortune and needed legal fees. *(Cyber: Mass-scale spear-phishing)*
- **The Diamond Hoax of 1872:** Conmen bought cheap diamonds and "salted" a field in Wyoming, fooling elite mining engineers. *(Cyber: Fake Proof-of-Work / Falsified audit logs)*
- **The Salad Oil Scandal (1963):** Filling holding tanks mostly with water and a tiny layer of oil on top to secure massive bank loans. *(Cyber: Asset spoofing / Modifying telemetry data)*
- **Victor Lustig’s "Rumanian Box" (1920s):** Selling a wooden box that supposedly printed $100 bills, but only printed one every 6 hours. *(Cyber: Malicious hardware / Fake crypto-mining rigs)*

## 🏛️ Category 3. Social Proof, FOMO & Fabricated Reality

> **Psychological Lever:** *Trigger: The "Halo Effect"—if everyone else believes it, it must be true.*

- **Gregor MacGregor & Poyais (1820s):** Inventing a fake country and selling fake land grants with glossy guidebooks. *(Cyber: Fake Initial Coin Offerings (ICOs))*
- **The South Sea Bubble (1720):** Mass financial hysteria driven by insider manipulation and government-backed FOMO. *(Cyber: Pump-and-dump altcoin schemes)*
- **Theranos & Elizabeth Holmes (2000s):** Masking a non-existent technology behind elite board members and strict NDAs. *(Cyber: Vaporware / Exploiting Non-Disclosure Agreements to prevent code audits)*
- **Anna Sorokin / Anna Delvey (2010s):** Exploiting elite financial institutions via lifestyle mirroring and fake wire transfer receipts. *(Cyber: Document forgery / In-transit data manipulation)*
- **Fyre Festival (2017):** Using influencer marketing to sell an entirely fabricated luxury experience. *(Cyber: Malicious apps boosted by bot-farmed fake reviews)*
- **Enron Corporation (2001):** Fabricating a reality of massive profits by hiding debt in off-balance-sheet shell companies. *(Cyber: Obfuscation / Logic bombs in financial software)*
- **Wirecard Scandal (2020):** Forged bank statements in the Philippines to "prove" 1.9 billion euros existed. *(Cyber: Forging API responses / Man-in-the-Middle reporting)*
- **OneCoin (2014):** A multi-billion dollar cryptocurrency scam that didn't actually have a blockchain. *(Cyber: Multi-Level Marketing (MLM) malware distribution)*
- **The Cardiff Giant (1869):** Buried a fake petrified giant; people paid to see it simply because crowds were gathering. *(Cyber: Click-farming / Manufactured virality for malicious links)*
- **Billionaire Boys Club (1980s):** A Ponzi scheme masked by the intense social proof of wealthy young men in high society. *(Cyber: Forum credential building / Hacking syndicates)*

## 🏛️ Category 4. Sympathy, Trust, Fear & Relationship Exploitation

> **Psychological Lever:** *Trigger: Bypassing logic by building emotional rapport, manipulating grief, or instilling panic.*

- **The Fox Sisters (1848):** Exploiting the grief of the bereaved via "cold reading" and fake supernatural tapping. *(Cyber: OSINT-based spear phishing targeting personal loss)*
- **Thérèse Humbert (1880s):** Living on credit for 20 years by claiming a locked safe contained a disputed inheritance. *(Cyber: Promissory token scams)*
- **The Grandparent Scam (2000s):** Calling elderly victims pretending to be a grandchild in jail in a foreign country. *(Cyber: AI Voice Cloning / Deepfake Vishing)*
- **Simon Leviev / Tinder Swindler (2010s):** Romance scam using money from Victim A to create the illusion of wealth to hook Victim B. *(Cyber: Multi-stage romance scams)*
- **The Cottingley Fairies (1917):** Two young girls fake photos of fairies; even Sir Arthur Conan Doyle believed it. *(Cyber: Confirmation bias / Deepfakes)*
- **Belle Gibson (2013):** Faked terminal brain cancer and claimed she cured it with diet to build a massive wellness empire and app. *(Cyber: Fraudulent GoFundMe / Sympathy phishing)*
- **Grigori Rasputin (1900s):** Gaining ultimate political insider access by exploiting the Tsar and Tsarina's desperation for their sick son. *(Cyber: Gaining lateral movement via compromised executives)*
- **John Brinkley / The Goat Gland Doctor (1920s):** Exploiting male insecurity and health fears via early mass media (radio). *(Cyber: Phishing via scareware / Fake antivirus pop-ups)*
- **The Badger Game (19th-20th Century):** Extortion honeytrap where a victim is lured into a compromising position, then "caught" by a fake angry husband demanding money. *(Cyber: Sextortion / Webcam blackmail emails)*
- **Pig Butchering / Sha Zhu Pan (Modern):** Industrial-scale relationship building via wrong-number texts, slowly funneling the victim into fake crypto investments. *(Cyber: Long-term socially engineered crypto theft)*

## 🏛️ Category 5. Diversion, Bypassing & Physical Pretexting

> **Psychological Lever:** *Trigger: Slipping malicious payloads past perimeter defenses via distraction or blending in.*

- **The Trojan Horse (12th Century BC):** The ultimate payload delivery system. Hiding a threat inside a trusted gift. *(Cyber: Trojan Malware / Malicious email attachments)*
- **The Antwerp Diamond Heist (2003):** Rented an office in the diamond center for 3 years to build trust and map the vault layout. *(Cyber: Long-dwell-time network persistence / Insider threat)*
- **The Great Train Robbery (1963):** Thieves manipulated railway track signals to turn red, stopping the train to rob it. *(Cyber: Man-in-the-Middle / Intercepting and altering routing protocols)*
- **D.B. Cooper (1971):** Using a briefcase bomb threat to force an airplane crew into compliance and bypassing landing protocols. *(Cyber: Ransomware / Distributed Denial of Service (DDoS) coercion)*
- **The Bank Examiner Fraud (1930s):** Posing as a bank investigator, convincing citizens to withdraw cash to "test" if the tellers were stealing. *(Cyber: Vishing / "Microsoft Tech Support" scams)*
- **Banco Central Burglary (2005):** Thieves set up a fake landscaping company next to a bank to provide a valid pretext for the dirt they were digging out of their tunnel. *(Cyber: Masking malware traffic as legitimate background telemetry/DNS requests)*
- **Operation Fortitude / Ghost Army (1944):** Using inflatable tanks and fake radio traffic to trick the Nazis into defending the wrong beach. *(Cyber: Decoy servers / Honeypots / Sensory spoofing)*
- **The Fake Night Drop Box (1970s):** Conmen placed an "Out of Order" sign over a bank's night deposit slot and left a basket next to it saying "Leave Deposits Here." *(Cyber: DNS Spoofing / Evil Twin Wi-Fi attacks / Credential harvesting pages)*
- **The MIT Blackjack Team (1990s):** Bypassing casino defenses not by cheating, but by card counting and rigid team coordination (spotters, gorillas, big players). *(Cyber: Bypassing anomaly detection / Distributed attack coordination)*
- **Vincenzo Peruggia Steals the Mona Lisa (1911):** Hid in a broom closet overnight, put on a white worker's smock, and walked out with the painting under his coat. *(Cyber: "Living off the Land" attacks / Blending in with normal employee traffic)*
