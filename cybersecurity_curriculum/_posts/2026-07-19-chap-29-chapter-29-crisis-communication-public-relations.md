*   **Breach Notification & Incident Public Relations:** Navigating the legal and public aspects of a security incident, adhering to regulatory disclosure timelines, coordinating closely with outside legal counsel, and drafting safe-harbor public announcements.
*   **Law Enforcement Coordination & Liaising:** Establishing and maintaining communication protocols with local, state, and federal agencies (including the FBI, CISA, and State Police) during active investigations of cybercrimes or systemic breaches.
