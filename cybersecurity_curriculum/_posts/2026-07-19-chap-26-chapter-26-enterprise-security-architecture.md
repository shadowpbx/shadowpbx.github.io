*   **The SABSA Framework (Sherwood Applied Business Security Architecture):** Designing and applying enterprise-wide security architecture using a structured, business-driven framework, mapping business context and concepts down to logical, physical, and operational security controls across multiple layers.
*   **TOGAF Integration (The Open Group Architecture Framework):** Aligning security-specific architectures directly with broader enterprise IT frameworks, ensuring security controls are natively baked into overall business technology designs.
*   **Mergers, Acquisitions, & Divestitures (M&A) Security:** Assessing and managing systemic cybersecurity risk during corporate transitions, performing due diligence on target networks, and securely integrating or cleanly separating disparate network infrastructures.
