*   **Zero-Day Discovery & Responsible Disclosure:** Managing the complex lifecycle of identifying previously unknown software flaws, adhering to ethical standards of Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure (CVD), interacting with vendor bug bounty platforms, and successfully registering Common Vulnerabilities and Exposures (CVEs) to help secure the broader ecosystem.
*   **Industry Mentorship & Community Engagement:** Giving back to the cybersecurity community by sharing knowledge, presenting technical research at major global security conferences (such as DEF CON and Black Hat), maintaining open-source security toolsets, and training the next generation of security professionals.
