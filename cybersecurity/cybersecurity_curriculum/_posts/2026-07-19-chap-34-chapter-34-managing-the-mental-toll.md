*   **Imposter Syndrome Management:** Navigating the psychological weight of operating in a fast-paced, highly technical industry where complete mastery is mathematically impossible, and developing systemic strategies to manage cognitive overload and self-doubt.
*   **Paranoia vs. Pragmatism (Risk Acceptance):** Balancing the adversarial mindset of extreme threat modeling (paranoia) with the commercial reality of corporate risk tolerance (pragmatism), and learning to define, accept, and secure systems within realistic residual risk boundaries.
