---

*   **Global & Regional Privacy Frameworks:** Complying with cross-border data protection requirements, managing European user data rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), navigating state laws like the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and enforcing data sovereignty boundaries.
*   **Healthcare & Financial Regulations:** Protecting highly sensitive vertical industries, auditing Protected Health Information (PHI) under the HIPAA and HITECH acts, and securing cardholder transaction networks using the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI-DSS).
*   **Government-Specific Security Mandates:** Hardening data networks holding public sector records, implementing Criminal Justice Information Services (CJIS) controls, protecting Federal Tax Information under IRS Publication 1075, and securing student academic databases under the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA).
*   **Policy & Standards Development:** Authoring, updating, and enforcing administrative security blueprints, including corporate Acceptable Use Policies (AUP), Incident Response Plans (IRPs), and corporate Data Classification Policies.
