*   **Satellite Constellation Architecture & Ground Stations:** Analyzing spatial computing security, comparing vulnerabilities across modern Low Earth Orbit (LEO) megaconstellations with static Geostationary (GEO) satellites, and securing terrestrial ground control stations against physical and network compromise.
*   **RF Signal Exploitation (GPS/GNSS Spoofing, Jamming, Telemetry):** Exploiting and defending over-the-air satellite communications, analyzing Global Positioning System (GPS) and Global Navigation Satellite System (GNSS) signal spoofing and jamming, and auditing unencrypted telemetry downlinks against passive eavesdropping.
