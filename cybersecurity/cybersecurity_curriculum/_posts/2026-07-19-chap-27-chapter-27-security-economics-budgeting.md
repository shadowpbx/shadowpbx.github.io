*   **Financial Models in Security (CapEx vs. OpEx):** Analyzing corporate security spending models, contrasting upfront capital investments (Capital Expenditures - CapEx, common in legacy on-premises hardware) with recurring service fees (Operational Expenditures - OpEx, common in cloud licensing and SaaS models).
*   **Security Procurement & Vendor Negotiation:** Designing robust procurement pipelines, authoring detailed Requests for Proposals (RFPs), establishing structured Proof of Concepts (PoCs) to validate vendor claims, and negotiating service level agreements (SLAs) to avoid vendor lock-in.
*   **Security Performance Metrics (KPIs & KRIs):** Measuring the performance and risk of the security program using Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) and Key Risk Indicators (KRIs), tracking core operational timelines such as Mean Time to Detect (MTTD) and Mean Time to Respond (MTTR).
