*   **Medical IoT (IoMT - Internet of Medical Things):** Securing network-connected and implantable medical hardware (such as pacemakers and insulin pumps) against remote execution bugs, unauthorized radio manipulation, and denial-of-service battery-depletion attacks.
*   **Genomic Data Security:** Securing centralized biological databases, protecting massive DNA sequencing repositories against unauthorized exfiltration, and preventing integrity-manipulation attacks on genomic records.
